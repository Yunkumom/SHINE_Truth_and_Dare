## 01 臺灣櫻花鉤吻鮭 / Formosan Landlocked Salmon

- Scientific name / 學名: *Oncorhynchus masou formosanus*
- Group / 類別: vertebrate / 脊椎動物
- Front line / 正面短語: `把冰河留下的勇氣，游進今天。` / `Carry an ice-age courage into today.`
- Back story / 卡背故事: 冰河退去後，這群鮭魚留在臺灣高山的冷水溪流，終生保有幼魚般的橢圓雲紋；牠對清涼、乾淨且連續的溪流極為依賴。 / After the ice age, this salmon population remained in Taiwan's cold mountain streams, retaining oval parr marks throughout life and depending on cool, clean, connected water.
- Blessing / 紀念祝福: `願你逆流時仍記得，自己來自很遠的勇氣。` / `May you remember your ancient courage whenever you swim upstream.`
- Source / 來源: [Taiwan Encyclopedia of Life](https://taieol.tw/pages/69338/articles)
