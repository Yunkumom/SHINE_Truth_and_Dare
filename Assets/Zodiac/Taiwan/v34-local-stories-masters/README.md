# Taiwan Zodiac Local Stories v34 Masters

Twelve original 1024 × 1536 PNG masters reinterpret the western zodiac through Taiwanese landscapes, ecology, crafts, transport and contemporary daily life. Animals and symbols lead where appropriate; human figures wear contemporary clothing. Every image includes a complete coloured Taiwan silhouette inside its governed crop.

12 張 1024 × 1536 原始 PNG 以台灣景觀、生態、工藝、交通及當代生活重新詮釋西洋星座。適合的星座以動物或象徵為主；人物皆穿著當代服裝。每張圖都在受治理裁切範圍內放置完整彩色台灣輪廓。

`manifest.json` records the short card label, longer educational note, prompt summary, master/runtime filenames and Taiwan placement. `contact-sheet-v34.jpg` is the inspection overview. v33 classical personified masters remain untouched in the sibling `v33-masters/` folder.
