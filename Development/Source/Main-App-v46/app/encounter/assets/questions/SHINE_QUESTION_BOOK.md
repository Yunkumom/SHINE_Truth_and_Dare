# SHINE QUESTION BOOK

## 真心話・連結問題集

完整雙語素材，共 62 張不重複卡片。遊戲深度依序為：暖身破冰 → 一星連結 → 二星連結 → 三星連結。

## ☆ BREAK THE ICE｜暖身破冰（9 Cards）

### 01. GRATITUDE CHECK｜感恩時刻
- EN: Share two things you’re grateful for today.
- 中：分享兩件你今天感恩的事情。

### 02. HOW DO YOU SHOW LOVE?｜你如何表達愛？
- EN: How do you usually express love in your daily life?
- 中：你平常如何在生活中表達「愛」？

### 03. GROUP SELFIE TIME!｜團體自拍時間
- EN: Take a group photo together and post it.
- 中：所有玩家一起合照一張，並上傳打卡。

### 04. SING IT OUT!｜唱出來！
- EN: Each player has 10 seconds to sing their favorite song.
- 中：每位玩家用 10 秒唱一首自己最喜歡的歌。

### 05. FUNNY FACE CHALLENGE｜鬼臉挑戰
- EN: Pick two players to make their funniest faces and take a picture together.
- 中：指定兩位玩家一起扮鬼臉，並拍一張合照。

### 06. PHOTO STORY｜照片故事
- EN: Open your phone, choose a recent photo, and tell the story behind it.
- 中：打開手機，挑一張最近拍的照片，分享它背後的故事。

### 07. WHO ARE YOU, REALLY?｜真實的你
- EN: How do you think people see you? Have you ever done something completely opposite to that image?
- 中：你覺得別人眼中的你是什麼樣子？你有沒有做過完全不符合這個形象的事情？

### 08. LABEL YOURSELF｜三個代表你的標籤
- EN: Think of three words that represent you and explain why you chose them.
- 中：想出三個最能代表自己的詞，並分享為什麼選擇它們。

### 09. THROWBACK TIME!｜童年回顧
- EN: Show everyone a childhood photo of yourself.
- 中：分享一張自己小時候的照片給大家看。

## ★ ONE-STAR CONNECTION｜一星連結（20 Cards）

### 10. MEMORABLE BOOK OR MOVIE｜難忘的書或電影
- EN: Is there a book or movie that left a deep impression on you? Why?
- 中：有沒有一本書或一部電影讓你印象非常深刻？為什麼？

### 11. INTROVERT OR EXTROVERT?｜內向還是外向？
- EN: Do you see yourself as more introverted or extroverted? Share an example.
- 中：你覺得自己比較偏內向還是外向？分享一個具體例子。

### 12. YOUR SOCIAL SUPERPOWER｜你的社交超能力
- EN: What’s your biggest strength in social situations?
- 中：你覺得自己在社交場合中最大的優勢是什麼？

### 13. REWIND TIME｜回到某個年紀
- EN: If you could go back to a certain age, which age would you choose? Why?
- 中：如果可以回到某個年齡，你最想回到幾歲？為什麼？

### 14. YOUR RELAXATION RITUAL｜你的放鬆儀式
- EN: What helps you relax or feel at peace the most?
- 中：什麼事情最能讓你放鬆或感到平靜？

### 15. FIRST IMPRESSIONS｜第一印象
- EN: Everyone shares their first impression of the person who drew the card.
- 中：每位玩家分享自己對抽卡者的第一印象。

### 16. SAY SOMETHING NICE!｜說句好話
- EN: Everyone shares something with the card-drawer: a thank-you, a compliment, or a blessing.
- 中：每個人對抽卡者說一句話，可以是感謝、稱讚或祝福。

### 17. FAVORITE CHILDHOOD CARTOON / ANIME｜童年最愛卡通／動畫
- EN: What was your favorite cartoon or anime as a kid?
- 中：你小時候最喜歡哪一部卡通或動畫？

### 18. INSTANT TALENT｜瞬間掌握一項才能
- EN: If you could instantly master one skill, what would it be?
- 中：如果你可以瞬間精通一項技能，你會選什麼？

### 19. A MEANINGFUL QUOTE｜影響你的一句話
- EN: Share a quote you love. How has it influenced you?
- 中：分享一句你很喜歡的話。它如何影響了你？

### 20. A FUNNY OR HAPPY MOMENT｜最近的快樂時刻
- EN: Share something that made you laugh or feel happy recently.
- 中：分享一個最近讓你覺得好笑或開心的時刻。

### 21. GO-TO HOBBY｜你的休閒首選
- EN: What’s your favorite leisure activity outside of work or study?
- 中：工作或學習之外，你最喜歡的休閒活動是什麼？

### 22. NEW HABIT TO ADOPT｜想培養的新習慣
- EN: What new habit would you like to integrate into your life?
- 中：你想要在生活中培養什麼新的習慣？

### 23. FIRST JOB EXPERIENCE｜第一份工作
- EN: What was your first job like? Any special memories?
- 中：你的第一份工作是什麼樣子？有什麼特別的回憶嗎？

### 24. KEY TO LASTING FRIENDSHIPS｜長久友誼的關鍵
- EN: What do you think is the most important factor in maintaining a friendship?
- 中：你覺得維持一段長久友誼最重要的元素是什麼？

### 25. UNFORGETTABLE CHILDHOOD MEMORY｜難忘的童年回憶
- EN: What’s a childhood memory that has stayed with you?
- 中：關於童年，你最難忘的一段記憶是什麼？

### 26. A LUCKY MOMENT｜幸運時刻
- EN: Talk about something that made you feel lucky recently.
- 中：分享一件最近讓你覺得自己很幸運的事情。

### 27. RECENT LIFE LESSON｜最近的人生體悟
- EN: Share something valuable you’ve realized recently.
- 中：分享一個你最近從生活中得到的重要體悟。

### 28. BEST TYPE OF GIFT｜最喜歡收到的禮物
- EN: What kind of gift do you love receiving the most?
- 中：你最喜歡收到什麼類型的禮物？

### 29. DINNER WITH A CELEBRITY｜和名人共進晚餐
- EN: If you could have dinner with any celebrity, who would it be? Why?
- 中：如果可以跟任何一位名人一起吃飯，你會選誰？為什麼？

## ★★ TWO-STAR CONNECTION｜二星連結（19 Cards）

### 30. SOMETHING YOU PROCRASTINATE｜你一直拖延的改變
- EN: What’s something you know you should change but keep telling yourself there’s still time?
- 中：有什麼事情是你知道應該改變，卻一直告訴自己「還有時間」？

### 31. OVERCOMING FEAR｜克服恐懼
- EN: Share a time when you faced and overcame a fear. What happened?
- 中：分享一次你面對並克服恐懼的經歷。當時發生了什麼？

### 32. WHAT MAKES YOU ANGRY?｜什麼最容易讓你生氣？
- EN: What behaviors upset you the most? How do you usually express anger?
- 中：什麼樣的行為最讓你生氣？你通常如何表達憤怒？

### 33. LAST LIE TO YOUR PARENTS｜最近一次對父母說謊
- EN: What was the last lie you told your parents, and why?
- 中：你最近一次對父母撒謊是什麼？為什麼？

### 34. SHOWING YOU CARE｜你如何表達在乎
- EN: How do you express care and affection? Do the people around you recognize it?
- 中：你會如何表達關心或在乎？身邊的人能感受到嗎？

### 35. ACKNOWLEDGE SOMEONE HERE｜欣賞現場的一個人
- EN: Choose someone here and acknowledge one of their qualities, actions, or a shared memory. How have they impacted you?
- 中：選擇現場一個人，分享你欣賞他的特質、行為或共同回憶。他對你產生了什麼影響？

### 36. HOW WOULD OTHERS INTRODUCE YOU?｜別人會怎麼介紹你？
- EN: How would the people here describe you to a stranger? Everyone answers for the card-drawer.
- 中：如果要把抽卡者介紹給陌生人，在場的人會怎麼形容他？由所有玩家回答。

### 37. FAVORITE PART OF LIFE RIGHT NOW｜現在生活中最喜歡的部分
- EN: What do you love most about your life right now? What need does it fulfill for you?
- 中：你現在生活中最喜歡的是哪個部分？它滿足了你內心什麼樣的需求？

### 38. FEELING UNHEARD｜不被聽見的時刻
- EN: Share a time when someone important to you ignored your feelings or needs. How did you react?
- 中：分享一次重要的人忽略了你的感受或需求的經歷。當時你怎麼反應？

### 39. LAST TIME YOU GOT EMOTIONAL｜最近一次紅了眼眶
- EN: When was the last time you teared up? What happened?
- 中：最近一次讓你紅了眼眶或想哭是什麼時候？發生了什麼？

### 40. FEELING UNDERSTOOD｜被真正理解
- EN: Has someone recently said or done something that made you feel truly understood?
- 中：最近有沒有人說了一句話或做了一件事，讓你感覺「他真的懂我」？

### 41. YOUR ROLE IN THE FAMILY｜你在家庭中的角色
- EN: How would you describe your role in your family? Do your family members see you the same way?
- 中：你會如何形容自己在家庭中扮演的角色？家人也是這樣看待你的嗎？

### 42. PERSONAL GROWTH｜你的成長
- EN: In what area have you grown the most? Was that growth inspired by a particular event or person?
- 中：你認為自己在哪個方面成長最多？這份成長是否來自某個重要事件或人物？

### 43. FROM HEARTBREAK TO SMILE｜從心碎到微笑
- EN: Is there something that once broke your heart but now makes you smile when you think about it?
- 中：有沒有一件事曾經讓你心碎，但現在回想起來已經可以微笑面對？

### 44. NO-JUDGMENT DREAM｜沒有評價的夢想
- EN: If nobody judged or opposed you, what would you most want to do or accomplish?
- 中：如果沒有人嘲笑、批評或反對你，你最想做成什麼事情？

### 45. A MOMENT YOU FELT SEEN｜被真正看見的時刻
- EN: Describe a time when someone truly recognized your strengths or qualities. What did they see in you?
- 中：描述一次你真正感覺「被看見」的時刻。對方看見了你哪些特質或優點？

### 46. IDEAL RELATIONSHIP QUALITIES｜理想關係的特質
- EN: What qualities do you think are essential for a great relationship? Why?
- 中：你認為一段理想的關係需要具備哪些特質？為什麼？

### 47. SHOW GRATITUDE｜向一個人表達感謝
- EN: Choose one player and tell them something you’re grateful for. Give them a hug if both of you are comfortable with it.
- 中：選一位玩家，告訴對方一件你感謝他的事情。如果雙方都願意，也可以給對方一個擁抱。

### 48. YOUR PROUDEST TRAIT｜你最驕傲的特質
- EN: What personal trait are you most proud of? Why?
- 中：你最為自己哪一項個人特質感到驕傲？為什麼？

## ★★★ THREE-STAR CONNECTION｜三星連結（14 Cards）

### 49. A CORE MEMORY WITH YOUR PARENTS｜與父母的核心回憶
- EN: Share one of your most memorable moments with your parents. Why has it stayed with you?
- 中：分享一個你與父母之間印象最深刻的回憶。為什麼這段記憶一直留在你心中？

### 50. YOUR CURRENT LIFE CHALLENGE｜目前的人生課題
- EN: What life challenge are you facing right now? What’s holding you back, and how do you plan to overcome it?
- 中：你目前正在面對什麼人生課題？是什麼阻礙著你？你打算如何突破？

### 51. A MEMORY YOU’D ERASE｜想刪除的一段記憶
- EN: If you could erase one memory, what would it be? If you could go back, would you do anything differently?
- 中：如果可以刪除一段記憶，你會選擇哪一段？如果能重新來過，你會做出不同的選擇嗎？

### 52. EXPERIENCES THAT REDEFINE YOU｜重新定義你的經歷
- EN: What kinds of experiences can make someone rediscover who they are? Share a turning point in your own life.
- 中：你覺得什麼樣的經歷會讓人重新認識自己？分享一個你人生中的重要轉折點。

### 53. A LIFE-CHANGING DECISION｜改變人生的決定
- EN: At the end of your life, what decision do you think you’ll be most grateful you made?
- 中：在人生最後回頭看時，你覺得自己會最感謝曾經做過哪個決定？

### 54. WHAT YOU VALUE IN RELATIONSHIPS｜你在人際關係中珍惜什麼
- EN: What do you most want others to appreciate about you in a relationship? Do you value those same qualities in others?
- 中：在人際關係中，你最希望別人珍惜你的什麼特質？你也同樣珍惜別人身上的這些特質嗎？

### 55. A RECENT GUILTY MOMENT｜最近感到內疚的事情
- EN: What’s something you did recently that made you feel guilty?
- 中：最近有什麼事情讓你感到內疚？

### 56. A DREAM LEFT UNFINISHED｜尚未完成的夢想
- EN: What’s something you’ve always wanted to do but haven’t yet? What’s stopping you?
- 中：有什麼事情是你一直很想完成，卻至今還沒有做到？是什麼阻礙著你？

### 57. FAMILY DYNAMICS｜家庭關係
- EN: What would you most like to change about your family relationships? What’s the biggest challenge to overcome?
- 中：在家庭關係中，你最希望改變的是什麼？最大的障礙或挑戰是什麼？

### 58. YOUR PERFECT HEALING DAY｜完美療癒日
- EN: What would your ideal self-care or healing day look like? What elements would make it truly restorative for you?
- 中：想像一個完美的療癒日，你會怎麼安排？哪些元素最能真正讓你恢復能量？

### 59. REBUILDING A RELATIONSHIP｜重新塑造一段關係
- EN: If you could reshape your relationship with someone, what would you change, and why?
- 中：如果可以重新塑造你與某個人的關係，你最希望改變哪個部分？為什麼？

### 60. YOUR BEST RELATIONSHIP TRAIT｜你在人際關係中的最佳特質
- EN: What quality are you most proud of in your relationships with others? How did you discover this strength?
- 中：在人際關係中，你最為自己哪項特質感到驕傲？你是怎麼發現這項優點的？

### 61. YOUR BRAVEST DECISION｜你最勇敢的決定
- EN: What’s the bravest decision you’ve ever made? How did you feel at that moment?
- 中：你曾經做過最勇敢的決定是什麼？當時的心情如何？

### 62. FINDING STRENGTH UNDER PRESSURE｜壓力下找到力量
- EN: How do you motivate yourself and find strength when facing pressure or difficult times?
- 中：面對壓力與挑戰時，你會如何給自己力量與信心？
