# Australian Native Species Keepsake Cards / 澳洲原生物種紀念卡

Date: 2026-08-26
Status: approved for Library artwork and copy production

## Purpose / 目的

Create a collectible 25-card Australian flora-and-fauna keepsake set that follows the Taiwan native-species deck's elegant field-journal language while remaining a separate reusable artwork and copy collection.

建立 25 張可收藏的澳洲動植物紀念卡，延續臺灣原生物種牌組的精緻自然史觀察誌語言，同時維持獨立且可重複使用的圖像與文案集合。

## Composition / 組成

- 15 vertebrates / 15 種脊椎動物
- 5 plants / 5 種植物
- 5 invertebrates / 5 種無脊椎動物
- Recognisable anchors include red kangaroo, koala, platypus, emu, golden wattle, and waratah.

## Physical and crop contract / 實體尺寸與裁切規格

- Final card size is 63 × 88 mm, the familiar Pokémon-style trading-card proportion, with `aspect-ratio: 63 / 88`.
- Each independent master is a text-free 1024 × 1536 RGB PNG; the taller 2:3 source provides bleed around a centered 63:88 crop.
- The animal or plant's diagnostic silhouette remains inside the central 82% width and 84% height.
- A recognisable, unlabelled Australia map is integrated into every scene as a quiet field-journal inset, watermark, shadow, stone, leaf, sand, water, or sky motif.
- The complete map, including Tasmania, remains inside x=102–922 and y=154–1228 on the 1024 × 1536 source; it must not touch the crop edge, frame edge, or lower caption area.
- The lower 18% remains visually quiet for future bilingual card copy.
- Habitat and paper texture extend to all edges as adjustable bleed.

## Visual language / 視覺語言

- Elegant natural-history field journal: watercolor, coloured pencil, fine indigo ink, warm fibrous paper, restrained ochre/eucalyptus accents, and delicate botanical marginalia.
- One principal species per image, shown in a plausible Australian habitat.
- The Australia map is subordinate to the species, legible at card size, and geographically recognisable; it is not a flag, logo, border, or decorative frame.
- No typography, generated labels, pseudo-writing, card UI, logo, watermark, postage-stamp treatment, Pokémon characters, Poké Balls, trade dress, or copied franchise artwork.
- No imitation of Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander dot painting, bark painting, cross-hatching, clan designs, body paint, rock art, ceremonial objects, sacred sites, or living Indigenous artists.

## Card content / 卡片內容

Front:

- artwork
- bilingual common name
- one very short memory line

Back:

- scientific name
- one concise factual species story
- one symbolic keepsake blessing
- source link and editorial limitations maintained outside the image

Truth, Dare, and Surprise prompts are deferred to a later owner-approved integration phase.
