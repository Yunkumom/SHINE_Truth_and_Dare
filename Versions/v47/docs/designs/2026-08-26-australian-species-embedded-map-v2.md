# Australian Species Embedded-Map Revision V2 / 澳洲物種情境融合地圖 V2

Date: 2026-08-26
Status: implemented; verification recorded on 2026-08-26

## Direction / 方向

Replace the repeated corner-map treatment in all 25 Australian flora-and-fauna masters with an organic Australia-map form that belongs to each species' action, food, body, or habitat.

將 25 張澳洲動植物主圖中重複出現在角落的地圖，全部改成自然融入各物種動作、食物、身體或棲地的澳洲輪廓。

## Non-negotiable visual rule / 不可妥協的視覺規則

- No floating cartographic inset, paper diagram, sky watermark, corner badge, map icon, or unrelated overlay.
- The Australia silhouette must be formed by a scene material the species plausibly touches, eats, inhabits, casts, carries, or displays: trampled earth, footprints, food leaves, bark grain, burrow soil, chest marking, fruit, water ripple, seagrass clearing, moss pool, flower cluster, leaf shadow, feeding mark, or web.
- The integrated form remains recognisable as Australia and includes Tasmania, but it may be softened by natural texture so it does not look mechanically pasted on.
- The map must have a clear physical relationship to the subject: beneath its feet, beside its mouth, on its body, in the water it swims through, or within the plant or habitat it occupies.
- The complete organic map remains inside x=150–874 and y=260–1200 on the 1024 × 1536 source, away from the centered 63:88 crop edge and outside the lower 18% copy zone.
- The species remains the primary subject; the embedded map is a discoverable second read.

## Cultural and franchise boundaries / 文化與品牌邊界

- Natural speckles, stripes, and biological colour may be shown accurately, but the map form must not imitate Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander dot painting, bark painting, cross-hatching, clan designs, body paint, rock art, ceremonial objects, sacred sites, or living Indigenous artists.
- Pokémon-like means physical card size only: no characters, Poké Balls, logos, borders, rarity symbols, energy icons, or franchise trade dress.
