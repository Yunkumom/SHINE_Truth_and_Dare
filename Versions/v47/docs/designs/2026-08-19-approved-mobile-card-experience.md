# Approved mobile card experience

## Goal

Make the 430 × 932 experience legible, friendly, and tactile: compact mode selection, the largest undistorted keepsake, a physical card draw, question-only play, and a seamless post-draw keepsake.

## Approved decisions

1. The mode-home headline is `破冰遊戲選擇`; explanatory eyebrow and paragraph are removed and the heading is moved upward.
2. Gameplay branding is English-only `TRUTH OR DARE` wherever the former product wordmark or card-back title appeared.
3. Direct keepsakes fill the safe mobile width while preserving `63 / 88`; their title is the artwork name, deity art adds a concise guardian meaning, and a subtle Taiwanese meander is inset inside a continuous rounded edge.
4. Every one of the 42 governed artworks has its own short, friendly, theme-matched humorous blessing, selected automatically with the artwork while still allowing preset or custom copy.
5. Live revealed cards show only the question text; category, title, activity, and blessing metadata are omitted.
6. The deck exposes three underlying edges; the top card tracks an upward gesture, springs back below threshold, commits and flips above threshold, and fades for reduced-motion users.
7. Card and question settings edit drafts; `取消` discards and `儲存並套用` applies the chosen part to the current card immediately.
8. The post-draw keepsake centers today’s question, accepts optional memory-only answer keywords, keeps a compact blessing, and places contact exchange in a collapsed single-column optional disclosure.
9. Post-draw card sections share one rounded outer surface with hairline dividers instead of separate boxes; local PNG/share remains local and contains optional keywords and selected contacts.

## Privacy and accessibility

Answer keywords, names, contacts, and uploads live only in component memory and local export processing; no persistence, analytics, account, telemetry, or backend transmission is added. All controls stay inside 430 px, use semantic buttons/labels, and reduced motion replaces movement with a short fade.
